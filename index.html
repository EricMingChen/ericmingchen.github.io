About Me

Hi there, welcome to my academic homepage! I am a PhD researcher in Education at the University of Bristol.

My doctoral research is fully funded and explores digital content creation in virtual reality (VR) to foster learner engagement and vocabulary acquisition.

Driven by a passion for technology-enhanced learning, my core research interests are highlighted below. Please feel free to get in touch for collaborations or academic discussions!

Research Interests

Computer Assisted Language Learning (CALL) Virtual Reality (VR) & Artificial Intelligence (AI) Innovative Pedagogical Design Second Language Acquisition & Vocabulary Consolidation
